Manage data on your device

Remove a wardrobe item

Open Wardrobe, choose an imported item, and remove it. This clears its saved metadata from Vesta; delete any separately saved copy from your photo library.

Remove a look

Open Looks, choose the result, and delete it from the app. Images already saved or shared outside Vesta remain under those destinations’ controls.

Camera and photo access

Use iOS Settings or Android Settings to change Vesta’s camera and photo-library permissions at any time.

Clear all local app data

Use your device’s app-storage controls or uninstall Vesta. This removes Vesta’s local preferences and wardrobe records but not copies saved elsewhere.

Submit a privacy request

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection, or an appeal where applicable. Email the privacy team and use the subject line “Vesta privacy request.”

privacy@your-domain.example

Include: your request type; country/state; device platform; approximate date and time of the relevant try-on; job ID if available; and a store transaction reference only if the request concerns a purchase. Do not attach your person photo to the first email.

Current server image files are configured to expire automatically within 24 hours. If your request is about a recent upload, contact us promptly and include the job ID.

How verification works

Vesta will ask only for information reasonably needed to match a request and protect against fraudulent deletion or disclosure. Because the current app has no Vesta account, Vesta may be unable to identify data after temporary files expire. An authorized agent must show authority to act for you; Vesta may also verify the request directly with you.

Response timing and appeals

Vesta aims to acknowledge requests promptly and respond within the period required by applicable law—generally one month under the GDPR and 45 days under several U.S. state laws, subject to permitted extensions. If a request is denied, the response will explain why and how to appeal where applicable. Appeal by replying with “Privacy appeal” in the subject line.

Sale, sharing, and targeted advertising

Vesta does not currently sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for targeted advertising. Vesta therefore does not provide a separate “Do Not Sell or Share” toggle in the current version. If this practice changes, Vesta will update this page and honor applicable opt-out signals.
