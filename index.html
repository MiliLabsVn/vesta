GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, fitted.

Does Vesta show an exact physical fit?

No. Vesta creates an AI-generated visual preview, not a sizing or tailoring measurement. Color, drape, body shape, and garment details may vary from the real item.

Which products can I try on?

Vesta is designed for clothing such as tops, bottoms, dresses, and one-piece garments. Shoes and accessories are not currently supported.

Where are my wardrobe items stored?

Imported wardrobe metadata and cropped product images are saved locally on your device. AI processing uses temporary server copies as described in the Privacy Policy.

Do I need an account?

No account is required in the current version. Credits and purchases are handled through Apple App Store or Google Play where available.
